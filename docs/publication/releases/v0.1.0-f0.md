## Recorded results

- Local community 27B/Q4 inference with 30/30 expected visible answers in the stated profile.
- BLOOM-560m split across two CPU servers on one computer, with parity on tested inputs and recovery after failure.
- Authenticated loopback QUIC transport with negative controls.
- 5,600 economic simulations: implemented accounting invariants preserved, fictional economic configuration rejected. No real TU issuance or payment.
- Official Qwen3-8B artifacts prepared, BF16 inference pending. Kimi K3 inspection and partial loading did not execute the complete model.

The project prioritizes models above 27B. These local and small-model experiments do not establish an integrated larger-model distributed service.
